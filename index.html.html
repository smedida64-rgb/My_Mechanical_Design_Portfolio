Bracket Form & Shape Optimization

Parametric form analysis of bracket geometry optimizing cross-sectional profiles for minimal weight while maintaining structural rigidity. Topology refinement conducted to eliminate stress concentration zones.

Analysis Metrics:

  • Radius Optimization: Fillet radii tuned for stress distribution (R=3.5mm)
